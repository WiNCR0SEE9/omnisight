Method and scope

Built to be checked

  • Real data. Daily rain and maximum temperature since 1991 for five cities, more than 13,000 days each, from the Open-Meteo Historical Weather API (ERA5 reanalysis, a gridded model estimate). Weather data by Open-Meteo.com, CC BY 4.0.
  • Every score shows its working. Three indicators, the points each one adds, and the 1991–2020 normal they are measured against.
  • The model is tested on years it never saw. It trains on 1991–2015 and is scored on 2016 onward. The normals use training years only, and automated tests confirm that no future data leaks in.
  • Unusual months are found two ways, and every flag lists the evidence behind it.
  • Scenarios are kept apart. Observed data, model output, simulation and your own assumptions are labelled separately.
  • Runs on a laptop with the saved data, without internet.

Scope

  • The risk score describes the last three months. It is an indicator, not a forecast or an official warning.
  • It uses rain and temperature only. Groundwater, reservoir levels and water demand are not included yet.
  • The weights (50% rain, 25% heat, 25% dry spell) and the level boundaries are design choices.
  • The next-month model is a research feature. Its measured skill is modest, and its test result is shown openly in the Next month section.
  • City positions are approximate points, not district boundaries.
